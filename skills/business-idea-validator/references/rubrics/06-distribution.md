# Rubric 6: Distribution

**Id:** `distribution`

**Question:** How will the first ten, then the first hundred customers find this, and what will it cost?

## What to look for

- **A concrete first channel.** A community the founder is in, a list they own, a partner who already sells to this customer, a search query with volume, an app store category, outbound to a list that can be built. "Social media and content" without specifics is not a channel.
- **Fit between channel and price.** Cheap products need cheap channels (search, virality, marketplaces). Sales calls only work when the yearly price pays for them.
- **Founder's unfair distribution.** An audience, a network, a brand, a past customer base. This is often worth more than the product.
- **Cost to acquire.** Even a rough sense: free (organic, referral), cheap (ads in a niche), expensive (outbound sales).
- **Repeatability.** Will the channel that brings customer one also bring customer fifty?

## Scoring anchors

- **5, Strong.** A named first channel the founder already has access to, matched to the price, with a plausible path to a repeatable second channel.
- **4, Solid.** A credible first channel is named; cost or repeatability is not addressed.
- **3, Unclear.** Channels are listed generically ("LinkedIn, SEO, ads") with nothing that shows access or fit.
- **2, Weak.** The channel does not match the price (sales calls for a 10 a month tool), or acquisition depends on virality the product has no reason to generate.
- **1, Red flag.** No way to reach the customer at all, or the only channel is owned by a competitor or a platform that bans this kind of product.

## Confidence

- **High** when the text names channels and the founder's access to them.
- **Medium** when the channel is implied by the customer and category.
- **Low** when you had to guess how anyone would hear of it.

## Traps

- "Product-led growth" is a result, not a channel.
- Two-sided ideas need two channels; score the harder side.
- A referral loop needs a reason to refer; "users will tell their friends" is not one.
- Paid ads for an unvalidated idea are an experiment, not a channel.

## Output

Quote whatever the text says about reaching customers. In `assumptions`, name the channel you think fits and why. In `questions`, ask where the founder could get ten conversations with the customer this month without spending money.
